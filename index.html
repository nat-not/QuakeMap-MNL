Methodology

Geographic graph

OSM ways are split at shared nodes and road endpoints. Edge weights sum geographic distances along each segment. Crossings connect only where ways share an OSM node.

Independent trials

For magnitude M, road probability is 0.04 + 0.08(M − 5), and mapped bridge probability is 0.10 + 0.13(M − 5). Both are clamped to [0, 0.8]. Each segment receives a seeded independent draw.

Surviving network

Graph traversal finds connected components. Tarjan’s algorithm identifies cut vertices and cut edges in O(V + E). Heap-based Dijkstra ranks reachable eligible destinations in O((V + E) log V) on this road network.

Model limits

The graph is undirected and sampled from selected road classes. Capacities, occupancy, and closures are scenario inputs. No engineering damage, actual shelter status, traffic, or safe travel is predicted.
